About the clinic

A calm, bright clinic in the Greenhills Centre, run by Zoe.

Hello, I'm Zoe

I'm the owner of AS Aesthetic Clinic in Walkinstown, Dublin 12. I'm a qualified dental nurse, phlebotomist, aesthetic practitioner and beauty therapist.

With over 18 years of experience in the beauty industry, I'm truly passionate about my work. We strive to provide natural enhancements while prioritising the individual concerns of our clients.

The clinic offers a full range of treatments, including teeth whitening, facials and dermal filler packages. Daniella from DB Beauty also provides luxury laser rejuvenation facials, laser hair removal, lash services and bespoke brow treatments.
